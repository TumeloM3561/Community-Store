SIMPLE PROCESS

How Community Store works

Learn more →
01

Create an account

Register as a student, faculty member, vendor or resident. Students and faculty use their university email.

02

Find or list products

Search listings, filter by category and condition, or add your own with a clear price and description.

03

Connect safely

Check seller reviews, use your cart and checkout, and rate the seller afterwards to help the next buyer.

TRUST AND SAFETY

Built so you know who you are dealing with

✅

Verified accounts

University email checks for students and faculty, and document checks for vendors.

⭐

Ratings and reviews

Buyers rate sellers after a purchase, so good sellers stand out.

🔒

Protected payments

Checkout is designed for secure payment gateways, with an extra check on high-value orders.

🛡️

Privacy first

Built with POPIA in mind: clear consent, and your data is only used to run the marketplace.
