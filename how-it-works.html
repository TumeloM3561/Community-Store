How Community Store works

Everything you need to know to start buying and selling safely.

FOR BUYERS

Buying in four steps

1

Create an account

Choose your role and register. Students and faculty use a university email.

2

Search and filter

Use keywords, category, condition and price to find what you need.

3

Add to cart and pay

Review your cart and check out. Orders above R1,000 need an extra check.

4

Rate the seller

After your order, leave a rating to help other buyers.

FOR SELLERS

Selling in four steps

1

Register

Vendors submit business details for approval. Others can start right away.

2

Create a listing

Add a title, price, category, condition and description.

3

Respond to buyers

Agree a collection time and place on campus.

4

Build your reputation

Good reviews bring more buyers to your listings.

ROLES

What each role can do

🎓

Student

Buy and sell, post on the board, rate sellers. Needs a university email (.ac.za).

🧑‍🏫

Faculty and staff

Everything a student can do, plus campus announcements. Needs a university email.

🏪

Vendor

Sell products or services at scale. Business documents are checked before listings go live.

🏘️

Resident

Buy from and sell to the campus community with a standard account.

🧑‍💼

Administrator

Approves vendors, removes harmful listings and posts, and suspends accounts that break the rules.

🤝

Everyone

Follow the community guidelines, be honest in listings and treat others with respect.

STAY SAFE

Safety tips

Before you buy

  • Check the seller's rating and past reviews.
  • Ask for extra photos or details if unsure.
  • Be careful with prices that look too good to be true.

When you meet

  • Meet in a busy public place on campus, in daylight if possible.
  • Test electronics before paying.
  • Report anything suspicious to an administrator.
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a university email?

Students and faculty must register with a university email ending in .ac.za. Vendors and residents can use any valid email address.

Does it cost anything to list an item?

No. Listing and browsing are free during the project.

Is the payment real?

No. This version uses a demo checkout, and no real money moves. A real payment gateway will be connected in a later stage.

Why was I asked for an extra code at checkout?

Orders above R1,000 need an extra confirmation step to reduce fraud. In the demo the code is 123456.

How is my personal information used?

Only to run the marketplace. You give consent when you register, and the project is designed around POPIA.

What if a listing is fake or unsafe?

Report it. Administrators can remove listings and suspend accounts.
