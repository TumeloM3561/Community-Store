About Community Store

Who we are, why we built it and where it is going.

THE PROJECT

A safer, simpler campus marketplace

Students, staff, vendors and residents often trade through informal social media groups with no identity checks and no structured payments. Community Store brings them onto one platform with verified accounts, searchable listings, a cart and checkout, ratings and a community board.

The project was built by a team of five students for Project Management 3 at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology, using Agile (Scrum) with a R0 budget.

Project objectives

  • Build a working marketplace prototype.
  • Verify student and vendor accounts.
  • Deliver listings, search, cart and checkout.
  • Test with real users and aim for a 75% usability score.
