Sell an item

Turn what you no longer need into cash, or offer a service to your community.

Tips for a good listing

  • Use a clear title that says what the item is.
  • Be honest about condition, including any damage.
  • Set a fair price. Check similar listings first.
  • Say where and when buyers can collect.
  • Meet in a public place on campus.

Who can sell?

You need an account. Vendor listings will be reviewed by an administrator once the database is connected.
